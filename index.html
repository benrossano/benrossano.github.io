Ben Rossano

I’m a PhD student at Massachusetts Institute of Technology, where I am co-advised by Nick Roy and Leslie Kaelbling. My research focuses on task and motion planning in a world of large pretrained models and how the structure of classical planning can be combined with the commonsense knowledge these models bring.

Previously, I completed my Master’s degree working with Jonathan How on multi-robot coordination in unknown and uncertain environments. Before that, I earned my B.S. in Robotics from the University of Michigan (Go Blue!).

In my free time, I enjoy music guitar piano , sports basketball soccer tennis golf skiing , and travelling 19 countries visited. Check out my pop/rock cover band, Seldom Chalant!
